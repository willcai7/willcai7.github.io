# Animes, Comics, and Games 

## Animes 

Watching animes plays an important role in my entertainment. I used to have a lot of DVDs. Later, I use MP4 player to watch all animes. Now, I use QNAP with [Plex](https://www.plex.tv/) to mange all of them. Here are some of my favorite series:

- **Future GPX Cyber Formula (高智能方程式)** >> This is a classic racing anime with very cool car designs.
- **Mobile Suit Gundam Unicorn (机动战士高达独角兽)** >> The animation is beautifully done, and the Gundam designs are uniquely striking.
- **Neon Genesis Evangelion (新世纪福音战士)** >> A cyberpunk-style work that weaves in various religious elements.
- **Haikyuu!! (排球少年!!)** >> A story about high school volleyball competitions. Compared to other sports anime, its setting feels much more realistic.
- **Your Lie in April (四月是你的谎言)** >> A coming-of-age love story set against a musical backdrop.
- **Cowboy Bebop (星际牛仔)** >> A stylish futuristic tale with outstanding music design.
- **Rick and Morty** >> A wildly imaginative sci-fi adventure full of sharp satire and humor.
- **Durarara!! (无头骑士异闻录)** >> An interesting blend of urban legends and high school life.
- **March Comes in Like a Lion (三月的狮子)** >> A warm and touching story about youth and emotional growth.
- **Samurai Champloo (混沌武士)** >> A masterful combination of music and animation.





## Comics
I learned watercolor painting in elementary school, and as I grew older, I became very fond of reading and copying manga. I once collected volumes 1 to 85 of Detective Conan, along with most of Doraemon. After moving to the U.S., I started reading e-comics. Currently, I use QNAP with [Kavita](https://www.kavitareader.com/) to manage all my manga. Below are some of my favorite series:

- **Doraemon S: Special Adventures (哆啦A梦 S: 特别冒险篇)** >> I love seeing Doraemon go on adventures with robot cats from all over the world. 
- **Dragon Ball (龙珠)** >> I love the romantic adventures in the early story arcs as well as the combat design in the later parts. 
- **Naruto (火影忍者)** >> A story about a ninja who dreams of becoming Hokage. 
- **BLEACH (死神)** >>  Compared to other battle manga where the main characters are cheerful and optimistic, Ichigo always carried a sense of helplessness and subtle detachment.
- **Inuyasha (犬夜叉)** >> Compared to the battles, I prefer the romance in this time-travel story.
- **The Prince of Tennis (网球王子)** >> I prefer the manga over the anime because the design of the character skills is more complete. 
- **Initial D (头文字D)** >> This manga made me realize how incredibly cool driving could be.
- **Yu Yu Hakusho (幽游白书)** >>  I love how Yoshihiro Togashi captures subtle facial expressions with such precision.
- **Level E (レベルE)** >> A completely unrestrained and wildly imaginative manga.
- **Alice in Borderland (弥留之国的爱丽丝)** >> While not the best mystery manga in my opinion, I deeply resonated with the protagonist’s emotional journey. 
- **Kekkaishi (結界師)** >>  Though it’s primarily a battle manga, it conveyed a hard-to-describe sense of tranquility to me.
- **20th Century Boys (20世纪少年)** >> This manga tells the story of a group of grown men who reunite to save the world, bound by a promise they made in their childhood.
- **Monster (怪物)** >> Another of Urasawa’s works. Dr. Tenma pursues Johan along the edge of good and evil, driven by unwavering resolve.
- **Billy Bat (蝙蝠比利)** >> Urasawa’s final major work. To me, the bat represents the spirit passed down through generations of manga artists. The good bat represents mainstream storytelling, the evil bat represents avant-garde styles, and together they form Urasawa’s ode to all manga artists in history—he believes manga creators have the power to make the world better.
- **Attack on Titan (进击的巨人)** >> This used to be my favorite manga. But its ending left me very disappointed. I came to realize that manga, as a relatively fast-paced literary form, often struggles to maintain narrative rigor, let alone provide answers to real-world societal contradictions.
- **Hikaru no Go (棋魂)** >> This is a very delicate and nuanced manga. After reading it, I tried to learn Go and followed professional Go tournaments for a long time. My favorite Go player is 	이창호( I Chang-ho, 李昌镐).
- **Bakuman (爆漫王)** >> Bakuman is a special manga. It gave me a glimpse into the world of manga artists and helped me understand the hardships and pressure of that profession.
- **Goodbye, Eri (再见绘梨)** >> Great work by Fujimoto Tatsuki. It features brilliant paneling, a dreamlike story, rebellious anti-manga design, and thought-provoking reflections on the motivations behind artistic creation.


## Games

I've been playing games since I was a small child. My mom bought a PC in her shopping mall and it accidentally had some game pre-installed. Unluckily, the game was in English, and I can barely understand English at that time. I spent a long time clicking buttons randomly and finally figured out how to play it partially. And this game is Startcraft. 

- **Startcraft** (1998)>> The first game I played. I only learned to use the Terran, since I cannot understand the logic of the Zerg or the Protoss without English. I learned to use the siege tank to destroy the enemy base when I was six. 
- **Warcraft 3: The Frozen Throne** (2003)>> After Startcraft, I found another similar game with better graphics and unique characters. The most importannt thing is it's in Chinese. Like Startcraft, I only use Hum and my favorite unit is the hero Paladin.
- **Counter-Strike 1.5** (2004)>> Around the age of 10, CS 1.5 was extremely popular in internet cafes. My cousin and I spent many hours learning how to use the AK and AR in the game. But my favorite weapon was the AWP.
- **Maple Story** (2003)>> This was the first large-scale online game I ever played. It introduced me to many basic elements of fantasy worlds, such as magic, life, weapons, and pets. I played as a mage.
- **Red Alert 2** (2000)>> After playing enough Warcraft, I also tried Red Alert. I especially liked the rocket soldiers.
- **仙剑奇侠传4** (2007)>> This is my favorite Chinese RPG.
- **DmC: Devil May Cry** (2013)>> The first action game I ever played. Its fast-paced combat was absolutely thrilling and unforgettable.
- **FIFA 13** (2012)>> I consider this the most classic FIFA title. I spent a long time playing career mode.
- **NBA 2K14** (2013)>> Also the most classic basketball game in my view. It took me a long time to finally beat LeBron and Kobe.
- **Dota 2** (2013)>> This is my favorite MOBA game. During pandemic, I spend a lot of time playing it to relieve stress and loneliness. I reached Divine rank (4600+ MMR) and mainly play the offlane position. My best heroes are Axe, Sand King, and Dark Seer. My favorite Dota 2 players are [Miracle-](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Miracle-) and [ana](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Ana).
- **The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim** (2011)>> My favorite RPG. I’ve used many mod management tools, and the best one in my mind is [Mod Organizer 2](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/6194).
- **Grand Theft Auto V** (2013)>> The GTA series was a big part of my childhood. I played GTA II, GTA III, GTA: San Andreas, and GTA V. Most of the time, I play as a good citizen in the game.
- **Yu-Gi-Oh!** (2000)>> I collected many fake Yu-Gi-Oh! cards and played it with my friends since I was a child. During my high school, my friend taught me to use [YGOPRO](https://ygopro.org/). I'm a Shaddoll player since then.
